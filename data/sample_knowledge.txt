Arize Phoenix is an open-source AI observability platform.

Phoenix provides tracing capabilities for LLM applications and helps developers inspect the execution of AI pipelines.

Phoenix supports OpenTelemetry and OpenInference for collecting traces from AI applications.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, commonly called RAG, combines information retrieval with large language models.

A typical RAG pipeline retrieves relevant documents from a knowledge base and provides those documents as context to an LLM.

RAG evaluation measures how effectively the system retrieves relevant information and generates accurate, grounded answers.
